Biography

Notes & press

Born in Sweden in 1997, Karolina Bengtsson has made a rapid passage from the competition circuit to a house ensemble — first the Oper Frankfurt Opera Studio in 2021/22, then the soloist company from 2023/24.

She trained at the Royal Danish Academy of Music in Copenhagen and at the Mozarteum in Salzburg, where Barbara Bonney guided her master’s work across opera, Lied and oratorio, and Wolfgang Holzmair taught Lied. Gaiva Bandzinaite prepared roles. Her first Pamina was sung at the Mozarteum in 2021; the part has since followed her to Frankfurt, alongside Papagena in Ted Huffman’s production.

In the house she has moved with unusual range: Silvia in a new Ascanio in Alba, Belisa in Fortner’s In seinem Garten liebt Don Perlimplín Belisa, the Young Shepherd in Tannhäuser, Barbarina, Aljeja in Janáček’s From the House of the Dead, Sophie in Werther, Anna in Vito Žuraj’s Blühen, and now Fiordiligi. Summer 2025 took her to the Festival d’Aix-en-Provence as Camille in Charpentier’s Louise and to the Innsbruck Festwochen der Alten Musik as Dori in Traetta’s Ifigenia in Tauride. She made her Bayerische Staatsoper debut as Clotilde in Norma, and has appeared at the Royal Swedish Opera as Barbarina.

Concert work sits beside the stage: Haydn’s Die Schöpfung, Brahms’s Ein deutsches Requiem, Bach’s St Matthew Passion, Pergolesi’s Stabat Mater, and Lars-Erik Larsson’s Förklädd Gud. In 2025 she debuted with the Swedish Radio Symphony Orchestra and the Gothenburg Symphony Orchestra, singing Orff’s Carmina Burana at Berwaldhallen. In 2018 Daniel Nelson wrote Döden och Julia for her; she premiered and later recorded it with Malin Broman and Musica Vitae.
